# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Owners of small perfume shops (the buyers of this template) and the shoppers those shops serve. Shoppers browse on phones and desktops, often in the evening, deciding on a bottle they cannot smell, so the page has to carry the scent through imagery, notes and trust. The job to be done: find a fragrance, save candidates, and check out without friction.

## Product Purpose

A frontend-only perfume storefront template (browse, product pages, cart, wishlist, login, signup, fake checkout, order history) that is sold to perfume shops and rebranded with their catalog. Success is a shop owner seeing it and thinking it already looks like a fragrance house, with the brand, products and prices easy to swap.

## Brand Personality

Refined, assured, warm. A quiet fragrance house rather than a retailer. It should feel luxurious and exclusive, and also confident and trustworthy: clear prices, plain checkout, nothing shouting for attention.

## Anti-references

- The generic Shopify-theme look: identical product cards, stock banners, badge clutter.
- Gaudy "luxury": heavy gold gradients, glitter, ornamental flourishes.
- The beige/cream "artisan" palette (warm paper, brass, oxblood, espresso) that every AI-built brand site shares.
- The neon/purple AI-startup look: glows, gradient text, decorative glass panels.

## Design Principles

1. **Restraint is the luxury.** Few elements, generous space, one accent used sparingly. Prestige comes from what is left out.
2. **Let the scent lead.** Product imagery, notes and names carry each page. Interface chrome stays quiet.
3. **Trust at the till.** Prices, totals and next steps are always plain and legible. Cart and checkout feel as calm as the home page.
4. **Rebrandable by design.** Brand name, palette, type and catalog live in a few obvious places so a shop can make it theirs quickly.
5. **Warmth through voice and detail.** Warmth comes from copy, typography and motion timing, not from beige backgrounds.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 text contrast (including muted gold and gray text on dark), visible focus states, full keyboard operation, labelled form fields with inline errors. Every animation has a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative. Do not rely on color alone for state (wishlist, errors, selection).
